Why Backstop

The Story Behind the Name

The name Backstop comes from a lifelong connection to baseball and the leadership lessons that came with it. As a catcher, coach and GM, Steve learned to see the whole field, support the people around him, adapt quickly and step into whatever role the team needs.

That same mindset has shaped his career. Leadership is less about title or credit than helping teams perform, giving people opportunities to grow and creating an environment where thoughtful risk-taking and learning from setbacks are part of the process.

Baseball is also a reminder that progress does not always require a home run. Sometimes the right outcome is simply getting on base, moving a runner over or making the productive out that helps the team advance. Serve the team, stay adaptable, learn and keep moving forward — that philosophy is at the heart of Backstop.
